DMCA / Removal Request

IGDesk Downloader is a tool for downloading public content and does not host any media on its servers. If you believe this service has been used to infringe your copyright, or you want content referenced by a link removed from our index/allowlist, please contact us.

How to submit a request

Email your removal request to:

dmca@example.com

(Replace dmca@example.com with your operational contact address before launch.)

What to include

  • A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) at issue on this service.
  • Your contact information (name, email, phone).
  • A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Our process

We review complete requests promptly. Because we do not host media, action typically consists of blocking the reported link/platform path from the service.
