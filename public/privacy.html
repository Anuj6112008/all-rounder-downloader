Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Summary

IGDesk Downloader is built to be minimal and privacy-respecting. We do not require accounts, do not track you for advertising, and do not store your downloaded files or the links you submit.

What we process

  • The link you submit is processed transiently in-memory to read public media metadata and deliver your file. It is not permanently stored.
  • Basic request data (such as your IP address) is processed by our hosting provider for rate limiting and security, as described in their documentation.

What we do NOT do

  • No user accounts, cookies for tracking, or third-party advertising.
  • No permanent server-side storage of your files or links.
  • No full-URL server logging beyond what is needed for error handling.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Local storage on your device

Your recent-history list and theme/language preferences are stored only in your browser (localStorage). History is opt-in, off by default, and never sent to any server. You can clear it at any time from the home page.

Hosting

The site is hosted on our provider's infrastructure, which may collect standard operational logs according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the contact section.
