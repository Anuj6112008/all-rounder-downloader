About IGDesk Downloader

IGDesk Downloader is a free, mobile-first web tool that lets you download public Instagram media: video in selectable quality (up to 1080p+ with audio), audio-only (native M4A/WEBM or converted MP3), and the best-resolution thumbnail — plus metadata like title, uploader and duration.

How it works

  • Paste a public link (Instagram Reel, post or story-video).
  • We read the media's public metadata using the open-source yt-dlp library.
  • You pick a quality/format; the file is delivered to your device.

What we don't do

  • We never ask for your cookies, passwords, or account credentials.
  • We don't use paid APIs, proxy rotation, or anything to evade platform protections.
  • We never bypass DRM or download private, login-only, or age-restricted content.
  • We don't store your files or links on our servers.

Public content only

Only download content you own or have explicit permission to download. You are responsible for respecting copyright and each platform's Terms of Service. See our Terms and DMCA pages.

Open source

This project is MIT-licensed and built entirely on free, open-source tooling. No API keys, no paid services.
