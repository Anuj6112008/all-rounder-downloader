Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms are supported?

At launch: Instagram (public Reels/posts) and YouTube (videos and Shorts). Other platforms (Facebook, X/Twitter, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Dailymotion) are listed as "coming soon" and will only be enabled once they pass live tests.

What quality can I download?

Whatever the source offers, up to the best available (including 1080p and above). Every listed quality includes audio — when the platform separates video and audio streams, they are merged automatically.

Can I get MP3?

Yes. The Audio tab offers MP3 at 128/192/320 kbps, plus the native audio (M4A or WEBM) so you can avoid re-encoding.

Where are my files stored?

Nowhere on our side. Files are streamed directly to your device and are not saved on the server.

Is this legal?

Downloading public content you own or have permission to download is fine. You must respect copyright and each platform's Terms of Service. Never download private, login-required, or DRM-protected content — the tool refuses those by design.

Why does a link fail?

Common reasons: the content is private/removed, requires a login, is geo-restricted, or the platform is rate-limiting. The error message tells you which. Private and login-only content will always be refused — we never use cookies or logins.

Do you track my history?

No. Recent history is optional, stored only in your browser, off by default, and never sent to a server.
